Welcome to Chili’s App

Two taps and it lives on your home screen — full screen, no browser bar, straight back to your cart.

Get it in three taps

Android phone

Chrome

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the corner.
  2. Pick Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install — the icon appears right away.
iPhone & iPad

Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari, then tap the Share button.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — you’re done.
Computer

Chrome

  1. Click the ⋮ menu in the toolbar.
  2. Choose Install Chili’s…
  3. Click Install — it opens in its own window.

Install from the browser you’re in: Safari on iPhone/iPad, Chrome on Android or desktop. Installed, the app runs full screen — no phone frame, no browser bars — and it still opens without a connection.

Just here to browse? Open the web app — no install needed.
